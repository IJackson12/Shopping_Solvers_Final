What does this show?

Monthly purchase rate

Purchase rate by month in the UCI dataset.

What does this show?

Highlights differences in purchase rates across the months represented in the dataset. It does not establish what caused those differences.

Weekday vs. weekend

Purchase rate for sessions by day type.

What does this show?

Compares the percentage of weekday and weekend sessions that resulted in a purchase.

Average product-related pages

Average ProductRelated pages by purchase outcome.

What does this show?

Compares average product page activity between sessions with and without a purchase.

Interpretation and limitations

These are descriptive comparisons from the UCI dataset. They show associations in the recorded sessions, not proof of cause and effect. Sessions without a purchase are not automatically confirmed cart abandonments.
